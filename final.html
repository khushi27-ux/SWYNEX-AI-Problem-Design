How it works

Pipeline

  1. Input is trimmed, capped at 6000 characters, and secrets are redacted.
  2. The parser finds the final error line, error type, message, and stack frames.
  3. A rule engine of 30+ patterns maps the error to a cause, explanation, 3 progressive hints, and a code fix.
  4. Confidence reflects how specific the match was. Unknown errors get a low score and honest generic advice.

Limits and ethics

  • It covers common Python and JavaScript errors. Rare or framework-specific errors fall back to general guidance.
  • It runs fully in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and history stays on your device.
  • Treat results as a learning aid. Verify fixes against your own code.
  • Hints come first on purpose, so you build debugging skill instead of copying answers.
